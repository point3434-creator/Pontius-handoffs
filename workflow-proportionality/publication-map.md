and acceptance record are unchanged. Original absolute local paths remain as
issued; this publication does not claim those paths exist on another machine.

reference to review-a-v2/review.md resolves here as support/review-a-v2/review.md,
and run-records/ resolves as support/run-records/. The complete reports, original
session logs and receipts retain their bytes. Canonical copies of the two valid
reports are also at r001/reviews/review-a.md and r001/reviews/review-b.md.

those original attributed reports, in A then B order. They are not new reviewer
verdicts. The controller's separate coordination history is support/progress.md;
repository-level progress.md records controller dispositions.

launch preflight are retained under support/. Their INCONCLUSIVE delivery

source and are not counted as completed cold passes. The two valid passes use
review-a-v2 and review-b-v2 only.

unchanged support/authorization-request.md. This publication grants no extra
source, operating, research or invocation authority. Candidate clones, fresh
test snapshots and process-temp lifecycles are retained locally, not copied
into this publication. The decision and push result will be issued separately

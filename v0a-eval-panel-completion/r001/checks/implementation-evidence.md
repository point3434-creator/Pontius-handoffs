working-tree checks or an implementer's report of success. Two implementers
owned disjoint library/test files; the coordinator owned orchestration/tests
and ran the integrated snapshot. None was a cold reviewer.

Initial teacher/export availability RED and its canonical board-name RED were

unexpected excluded result. It is raw diagnostic output, including long frames.

wrong ordinal and wrong hand each escaped rejection. The correction ties each
outcome to its scheduled ordinal, hand, label and witness. That initial output
is retained in tool history only. The final frozen test exercises all three.

instead of the actual nested result. That test error was corrected; it was not
a production defect. The final real-host test now includes actual transport
failure and a reason-only mutation of the retained successful CHECK stream.

and frame payloads are exempt from the authored-text layout gate. Authored
packet prose and changed source are LF, BOM-free and at most 100 columns.
Machine JSON, digest manifests and raw receipts retain their actual structure.

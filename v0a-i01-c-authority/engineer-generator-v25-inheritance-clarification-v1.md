# V25 inherited negative-obligation clarification

Engineering-only append-only clarification of v1/v2/v3. Root explicitly approved
this bounded direction after source review. No new witness family, payload,
candidate import, positive inheritance feature or MRO/shadow heap is authorized.

A copied base-member bundle is insufficient: a later Base.pending store must
remain visible to a subsequent Child.pending consumption. Retain a named
read-only base-owner edge in the existing helper-obligation channel at enabled
normal class installation. Its payload keeps the base value/ref, not a snapshot
of its member table. The central transfer boundary retains that edge without
executing a base/member/callable body or reconstructing missing cells.

Only member selection follows this edge, carrying the requested name through
the walk. It resolves current tagged authority records and collects that name's
may-roots plus wildcard roots. Role-aware value/authority visits are scoped to
the lookup; a base visit cannot suppress a receiver alternative or another name.
The existing ordered may-union retains alternatives; it creates no member
existence, positive return value, callable, descriptor or MRO proof.

Writes, ordinary immediate iteration and ordinary callable-capture traversal do
not follow the base edge. In particular, Child.pending writes are not redirected
to Base, and instance writes are not redirected to their class. A merely dormant
subclass/base/member is not refused. Unrelated member names remain unrelated.

Preserve an already-proved own-member result from the existing lookup path.
Otherwise same-name inherited may-roots are conservative: no new shadow-tracking
heap or MRO exclusion is added to manufacture clean outcomes. If an unresolved
or missing base edge cannot retain its member obligation, export an explicit
helper-namespace refusal obligation at the reached lookup/consumption boundary;
do not pretend the missing base is empty. Mere construction does not execute or
refuse it.

All additional edge allocation, visits, record reads, ordered dedup and copying
use the current operation budget; no new cap/epoch/backend. Original44 plus L8
expectations stay fixed. Any conflict with required-clean behavior is reported,
never relabeled after execution.

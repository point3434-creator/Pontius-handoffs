## Invariants and discriminating cases

1. Identity/authority: exactly two document changes over the declared parent;
   manifest hashes stored blob bytes with whole-row sort and LF. Existing source
   and retained records stay unchanged. Later reviews/runs need their own authority.
2. Root/domain: public replay reaches one hero root at s=4; board/hole/history,
   stack, contribution and return fields agree across teacher, exporter and host.
   Cases: altered stack/prefix; reversed board; collisions; folded private cards.
3. Numeric truth: both independent forced-action expectations reconstruct unique
   integer totals; best-response map/value is independently legal and consistent.
   Cases: all-zero tie, cancellation tie, false production tie, changed total,
   smallest positive/negative gap, wrong non-tie choice, nonfinite/map/domain error.
   Binary64 error proof must match actual normalization and accumulation operations.
4. Cost: reference construction, both forced evaluations, best response and compare
   all count. No joint full-range game; no automatic full solve after preflight.
   Cold/warm ordering, observed memory, finite limits and interrupted results matter.
5. Export: immutable teacher input; deterministic bytes; real codec wire limit;
   exact decoded key/action equality and complementary unsupported root set.
   Cases: zero/all-domain capacity, next prefix, duplicates, source-id width,
   solved artifact recheck, canonical versus wire size, CHECK versus raise size.
6. Witness law: every required h gets an actual seeded compatible twelve-card draw.
   No forced private cards or membership-based redefinition of H; census decides
   finite-bank completeness. Correctness witnesses never become Slice B samples.
7. Outcomes first: nested session result and real framed host closure decide
   completion; nullable failed deliveries, accepted-then-failed observations,
   missing result/settlement, truncation and malformed frames cannot become hits.
   One river record is an agreement requirement, not general chip eligibility.
8. Agreement: action and retained v1 reason agree with independent replayed lookup.
   CHECK hit/default distinction and separate public provider behavior both matter.
   Pre-river defaults do not inflate root counts; each attempt has one disposition.
   Completed prefix-diverged baselines preserve chip eligibility for later Slice B.
9. Ownership: one admitted root/context and one parent result/journal; real sessions
   still prepare and spawn their child. Root mismatch, launch failure, child failure,
   transport cleanup and inherited finish paths must not create duplicate run rows.
10. Verification/budget: test manifest registration, real boundaries where claimed,
    explicitly labeled fixtures and arithmetic, supported interpreter order, strict
    finite plans and inherited 600/400-line plus two-round allowance.
